Our story

The long way round

Two people from opposite corners of India, who somehow had to move to Germany to find each other. We started as classmates, ended up as flatmates, became friends somewhere in the middle of that, and then took an embarrassingly long time to admit the obvious.

The years since have been mostly chaos: exams, visas, three cities, one very small kitchen, and a standing argument about whose turn it is to call home.

Sneha is Marwari, from Rajasthan, where affection is measured in ghee and dinner may well open with something sweet. Yash is Maharashtrian, from Mumbai, where affection is measured in vada pav and the answer to most questions is more chilli.

She has since learned what a proper misal does to a person. He has learned that dal baati is not a suggestion. Neither of us has won the onion argument, and we no longer expect to.

In January her side arrives with dhol and ghoomar, his side with dhol-tasha, and both will be certain the other is playing too loud. Come and pick a side. There will be more than enough food to settle it.

With love, the Pisat & Khirwal families
